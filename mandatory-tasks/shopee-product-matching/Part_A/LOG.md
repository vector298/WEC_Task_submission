## 2026-10-01: why do different products share a phash?
Hypothesis: collisions come from plain white backgrounds, which make
different products look alike to phash.
Setup: printed titles and rendered photos for several colliding phashes.
Result: the photos were identical, and the titles were size or model
variants (kelambu 150x200 vs 180x200, Somethinc 20ml vs 40ml).
Observation: the guess was wrong. Sellers reuse one photo across
variants that Shopee labels as separate products.
Next: check the group-size distribution and what a threshold must handle.
